  • General Information.
    - Born October 26, 1965, in Trieste, Italy.
    - 1984: Diploma at I.T.C. "Gian Rinaldo Carli", Trieste.
    - 1985-86: Cadet at the Italian Air Force Academy (Eolo IV) as an engineering student.
    - 1987: Admission to the Scuola Normale Superiore as a student in Mathematics.
    - 1991: Degree in Mathematics at the University of Pisa. Thesis: Tempo ottimale per sistemi lineari autonomi in spazi di Hilbert. Advisor: prof. Giuseppe Da Prato.
    - 1991: Diploma in Mathematics at Scuola Normale Superiore, Pisa.
    - 1996: Ph.D. in Mathematics at Scuola Normale Superiore, Pisa. Thesis: Periodic points for expanding maps and for their extensions Advisor: prof. Edoardo Vesentini.
    - 1999-2017: Researcher in Mathematical Analysis at the Tor Vergata University of Rome.
    - 2017-now: Associate professor in Mathematical Analysis at the Tor Vergata University of Rome (National Scientific Qualification 2012 - 01/A3).

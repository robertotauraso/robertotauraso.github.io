American Mathematical Monthly Problems

The American Mathematical Monthly is an expository journal intended for a wide audience of mathematicians, from undergraduate students to research professionals. Each issue has a Problems and Solutions section where new problems are proposed.
A good portion of solutions to problems from past years can be found in the book More Monthly Problem Gems, which I had the pleasure of co-authoring with Hongwei Chen.
